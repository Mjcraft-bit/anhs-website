Welcome to Andarayan National High School! We are proud to be a place where students learn, grow, and discover what they can become. Our school is built on care, respect, and the belief that every learner deserves the chance to shine and build a bright future.

Our Mission

To give every student the best education possible — one that is fair, helpful, and truly makes a difference. We teach more than just lessons: we build good character, useful skills, and the confidence to do well in life. We work together with families and the community to help every young person reach their dreams.

Our Vision

We see Andarayan National High School as a place of excellence where students become honest, kind, and capable leaders. Our graduates leave ready to help their families, serve our community, and make the world a better place — today and always.
