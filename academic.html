Academic Programs

Senior High School strands, enrollment information, and class schedules.

Available Strands

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

Focuses on advanced math, sciences, and technology. You will study physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering concepts. Best for those aiming to become doctors, engineers, researchers, programmers, or scientists.

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management

Covers bookkeeping, business math, marketing, economics, and management. Learn how to start and run a business, handle finances, and lead teams. Ideal for future accountants, managers, entrepreneurs, and business professionals.

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

Explores human behavior, culture, politics, and communication. Develop strong writing, speaking, and critical thinking skills. Prepares you for law, education, journalism, psychology, public service, and social work.

GAS

General Academic Strand

A flexible choice that combines subjects from different strands. Perfect if you are still deciding on a specific career path. Gives you a balanced foundation and more time to discover what fits your interests and goals best.

TVL

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

Focuses on practical skills you can use right away. Hands-on training in areas like ICT, home economics, or industrial arts. Earn national certificates that help you find work or start a business immediately after graduation.
